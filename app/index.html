Security model

What Ballast can and cannot do

  • Ballast never holds your collateral outside a single atomic protection transaction.
  • Keepers can call protection, but cannot choose values outside your policy.
  • Your policy caps trigger, target, collateral sold, slippage, keeper fee, and cooldown.
  • Authorization is separate from policy setup and can be revoked on Morpho.

Trust assumptions

Read this before enrolling

  • The deployed manager owner can change the swap adapter; treat that owner as trusted until governance or a timelock is in place.
  • The contracts are not audited. Start with small exposure and conservative limits.
  • Protection depends on Morpho state, oracle updates, keeper uptime, and available Flare liquidity.
  • A policy is not a guarantee against liquidation or adverse execution.
