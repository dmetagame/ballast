Ballast

Automated, non-custodial deleveraging for XRP-collateralised borrow positions on Flare.

  • The contracts are not audited.
  • The manager owner can change the swap adapter. Treat that owner as trusted until governance or a timelock is in place.
  • Liquidity is the binding constraint. Deleveraging beats liquidation only up to roughly 300k FXRP in a single sale.
  • Protection depends on Morpho state, oracle updates, keeper uptime, and available Flare liquidity. A policy is not a guarantee against liquidation.
